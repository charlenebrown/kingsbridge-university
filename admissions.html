Plan with confidence.

How is financial need considered?

A financial aid review considers family circumstances alongside the cost of attendance. Speak with an advisor before making assumptions about eligibility.

What should transfer students prepare?

College transcripts, a statement of academic interests, and information about completed courses help inform the review of transfer credit.

Can I visit before applying?

Start with our virtual campus tour. Explore teaching spaces, campus life, and the community commons at your own pace.
